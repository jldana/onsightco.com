About This Project

Why we're collecting this data

This app lets citizen scientists log water and algae observations from the field. Entries include photos, GPS location, timestamp, and notes so researchers can track conditions over time.

Your data

Entries are saved on your device until you upload them. Nothing leaves your phone automatically.
