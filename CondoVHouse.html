A planning estimate, not advice. It assumes a fixed-rate loan, private mortgage insurance at 0.5% of the loan per year until the balance falls to 80% of the purchase price, and property tax, maintenance and appreciation applied to the home's current value. Insurance, HOA dues and utilities rise with the inflation setting. Renting is charged only for rent, renters insurance and utilities, and is assumed to have the same down payment cash invested elsewhere, which the owning columns already account for. No tax deductions are included.
